🚨 The Problem

Food Waste is a Global Crisis

Every day, millions of kilograms of edible food are discarded while millions go hungry. This is both a humanitarian and environmental emergency.

🗑️

1/3 of Food is Wasted

Approximately 1.3 billion tonnes of food is wasted globally every year — enough to feed 3 billion people.

🌡️

Climate Impact

Food waste contributes to 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions, making it a major driver of climate change.

💸

Economic Loss

India alone wastes food worth ₹92,000 crore annually. Restaurants, hotels, and households are the top contributors.

🍽️

Hunger Persists

Over 820 million people worldwide face hunger while surplus food sits in landfills. The gap can be bridged.

🏨

Hospitality Waste

Hotels, canteens, and events are among the largest contributors to preventable food waste — often daily.

🌱

The Solution Exists

With better coordination between donors, NGOs, and volunteers, most surplus food can be redirected effectively.

⚙️ How It Works

Simple. Fast. Impactful.

EcoTrack makes food redistribution effortless with a 4-step process.

1
🍱

Donor Lists Food

Restaurants, hotels, households list their surplus food with details, quantity, and pickup address.

2
🤝

NGO Requests

Verified NGOs browse available donations, filter by location/category, and send pickup requests.

3
🚗

Volunteer Picks Up
