About EcoTrack

We are on a mission to eliminate preventable food waste by building bridges between surplus food and communities in need.

🎯 Our Mission

Turning Surplus into Sustenance

EcoTrack was built to solve a fundamental disconnect: millions of kilograms of perfectly edible food goes to waste every day while millions of people go hungry.

Our platform creates a verified, transparent ecosystem where restaurants, hotels, canteens, events, and households can list surplus food. Verified NGOs can browse, request, and coordinate pickup. Trained community volunteers complete the last-mile delivery.

Every step is tracked. Every kilogram saved is measured. Every meal enabled is counted — because impact should be visible.

🇺🇳 SDG Alignment

Built for the Goals that Matter

EcoTrack is purpose-built to contribute to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

12

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption

Directly reducing food waste at source. Building sustainable consumption habits across restaurants, hotels, households, and events.

2

SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

Redirecting surplus food to NGOs serving food-insecure communities. Turning waste into nutrition for those who need it most.

13

SDG 13 — Climate Action

Food waste in landfills produces methane — a potent greenhouse gas. Redistribution directly reduces emissions and supports climate goals.
