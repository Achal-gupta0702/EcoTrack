How It Works

EcoTrack coordinates food redistribution through a transparent, multi-stakeholder process. Here's exactly how it works.

📋 Donation Status Flow

7-Stage Tracking System

Every donation passes through 7 stages — ensuring complete transparency from listing to delivery.

🟢 Available → 🟡 Requested → 🔵 Accepted → 🚗 Pickup Assigned → 📦 Picked Up → 🚚 Delivered → ✅ Completed
🍱 For Donors

How Donors Use EcoTrack

Restaurants, hotels, canteens, households, and event organizers can list surplus food in minutes.

1

Register & Create Account

Sign up as a Donor with your name, contact details, organization name, and city. Verification is instant.

2

List Surplus Food

Fill in the donation form with food name, category, quantity, expiry date/time, pickup address, and description.

3

Review & Accept NGO Requests

When an NGO requests your donation, you'll see it on your dashboard. Accept the request to move it forward.

4

Track Until Completion

Monitor your donation through all stages. See when it's picked up, delivered, and completed — creating your impact record.

🤝 For NGOs

How NGOs Use EcoTrack

Registered NGOs can browse, filter, and request surplus food from verified donors.

1
📝

Register as NGO

Create an NGO account with organization name, city, and contact details.

2
🔍

Browse & Filter

Search available donations by city, food category, and quantity. Find the most relevant listings.

3
📬

Request Donation

Send a pickup request for donations you need. The donor will review and accept your request.

4
🏁

Receive Food

Once a volunteer picks up and delivers, the food arrives at your NGO. Track every step.

🚗 For Volunteers

How Volunteers Make It Happen

Community volunteers are the backbone of EcoTrack — they bridge the last mile between donors and NGOs.

1
👤

Register as Volunteer

Sign up with your name, city, and contact details. No special requirements — just a willingness to help.

2
📋

View Available Pickups

Browse accepted donations in your area that need volunteer pickup. See full details including address and quantity.

3
✅

Accept & Complete

Accept a task, pick up the food from the donor, deliver to the NGO, and mark as delivered. Done!
